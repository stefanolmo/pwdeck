Word lists used by the passphrase generator
===========================================

eff_large.txt  - "EFF Long Wordlist" (7776 words, designed for passphrases) by the Electronic Frontier Foundation,
                 https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases
                 License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/
                 Distributed here without the dice numbers (one word per line), otherwise unmodified.

italian.txt    - Italian word list taken from the `xkcdpass` 1.30 package ("ita-wiki"), which generated it from dumps of the Italian
                 Wikipedia. License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
                 Modified by PW Deck: only lowercase ASCII words of 5-9 letters are kept (no accents), sorted, de-duplicated.
                 The list is a frequency list and therefore contains some proper nouns and foreign words; entropy depends only on its size.
                 This file remains under CC BY-SA 3.0; it is separate from PW Deck's own code (MIT).
